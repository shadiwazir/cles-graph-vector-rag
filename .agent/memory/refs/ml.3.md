## Update: first real build (2026-11-15/16, user approved)
- Build on 15 samples with gpt-5.4-mini: 59 examples (47 train, 12 eval), 59/59 targets ok on first pass, 0 leakage drops, check_dataset 0 violations, 171 s, cost 0.534 (+0.016 for 2 regenerated) at unverified prices (about $0.009 per example; est was 0.89). Likelihood cap visible: weak->low (12), moderate->medium (38), strong->high (9).
- Review findings: (1) writer once wrote "the issues identified in the hindsight" (mentions its briefing): added check forbidding "hindsight"/"actual next event"; cached targets are now re-validated every build (2 regenerated). Two other "ultimately" hits were verbatim quotes from other decisions (false positives). (2) 24/59 examples have no authorities (thin context); (3) first-route outcomes: 35 dismissed, 16 allowed, 8 allowed_in_part (corpus base rate; model will learn a dismissal bias; corpus has appeals only: selection bias); (4) STAGE SEMANTICS PROBLEM: the first route must equal the appeal's real disposition even for the facts/procedural stages, so early-stage targets are contrived (e.g. 2018 ONCA 238 facts-stage route "Reviewing court restores the first degree murder charge ... appeal_dismissed"; 708 facts-stage "Force majeure clause applies; appeal dismissed after no reversible error"). Open question for the user: options A keep (eventual appellate disposition at all stages, phrase early routes as 'if the matter reaches the Court of Appeal'), B restructure early-stage advice around next procedural paths (needs schema change), C train outcome-labelled targets only for lower_court_decision/grounds_of_appeal and use outcome-free targets for facts/procedural stages.
