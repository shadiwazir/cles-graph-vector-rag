About CLES Advisor

How an answer is made

  1. Understand the stageWhere does the case stand: facts, proceedings, a decision, grounds of appeal?
  2. RetrieveRelevant passages from earlier decisions, found by meaning and by keywords, plus a graph of how cases cite and treat each other.
  3. ReasonA fine-tuned language model proposes routes, likelihoods, risks and next steps, using only that material.
  4. VerifyEvery cited case, paragraph and quote is checked against the decision text. Citations that fail are removed.

Limits you should know

  • It supports a qualified lawyer's judgement. It is not legal advice.
  • It knows only the decisions in its knowledge base, and the law may have changed since.
  • Likelihoods are capped by the strength of the evidence found; weak evidence is shown as such.
  • Outcome prediction is hard: in the latest evaluation it was not clearly better than a general model with retrieval. See the results explorer.
